# Location-Based Offline Tuition Marketplace

## One-liner
Help students find nearby home tutors by subject and budget, and give teachers a way to manage their availability and incoming student requests.

## Value Proposition
Students currently struggle to find qualified home tutors in their area—they rely on word-of-mouth, scattered ads, or expensive tutoring centers. Teachers have no centralized way to connect with students seeking their expertise. A location-based marketplace solves this by making tutor discovery fast and local, while giving teachers control over their profile, availability, and incoming requests. This creates a direct, efficient connection between supply and demand.

## Who It's For
- **Students**: Find qualified tutors nearby by subject and budget without endless searching or middlemen
- **Teachers**: Connect directly with students seeking their expertise and manage requests and availability in one place
- **Admins**: Oversee users and maintain platform integrity through basic user management tools

## Job to Be Done
When I need a tutor for a specific subject in my area, I want to search by location, subject, and budget so I can quickly find someone qualified and nearby. When I'm a teacher offering tuition, I want to manage my profile, set my availability, and respond to student requests so I can build a sustainable tutoring practice.

## Key Functionality
- Modern landing page with search functionality
- User registration and login with Student and Teacher role selection
- Student dashboard for searching tutors by subject, location, and budget
- Student ability to send requests to tutors
- Teacher dashboard for managing their profile and availability
- Teacher dashboard for viewing and responding to incoming student requests
- Basic admin panel for user management
- SQLite database backend with Flask
- Responsive card-based UI design

## Out of Scope (Not in v1)
- **Payment processing**: Transactions handled offline; payment integration deferred until platform reaches scale
- **Advanced matching algorithms**: Manual search and discovery in v1; AI-powered recommendations come later
- **Mobile app**: Web-based experience sufficient for launch; native mobile apps deferred
- **Ratings and reviews**: Focus on core matching first; reputation system added after user base grows
- **Real-time notifications**: Basic request/response flow; push notifications and live updates deferred
- **Multi-language support**: English-only for v1; localization comes after market validation
- **Verification and background checks**: Trust built through basic profiles; formal verification deferred
- **Advanced analytics**: Basic admin panel only; detailed dashboards and reporting deferred
